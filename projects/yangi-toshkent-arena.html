FOOTBALL STADIUM / NEW TASHKENT / BAHADIR KUL ARCHITECTS

A football stadium for New Tashkent: a ring of tall white fins with glowing arched openings, raised on an elevated promenade that wraps the bowl and carries fans in from every side. A large screen carries the Uzbekistan Football Association crest.

The set moves from the masterplan and the long approach past the water and golden sphere, through the façade by day and at dusk, to match atmosphere inside under a membrane roof.
