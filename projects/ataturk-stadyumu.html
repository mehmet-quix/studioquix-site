NEW ATATÜRK STADIUM / LEFKOŞA, KKTC / BAHADIR KUL ARCHITECTS

A replacement for Lefkoşa's Atatürk Stadium: a compact bowl wrapped in folded, perforated white triangular panels, banded with red light and marked by a large crescent and star over the main entrance.

The set covers the stadium at every scale — its place in the city beneath the Beşparmak mountains, the plaza and solar-canopy parking, the entrances on match night, and the atmosphere inside the stands.
